feh, nomacs, Eye of GNOME, Gwenview, qimgv... I've tried dozens of Linux image viewers over the last decade, and something was always missing.

One wouldn't start fullscreen. Another wouldn't close on a single press of Escape. Others felt sluggish.
Finally, the day came - I built my own :D

Meet Better Image View (`biv`): a deliberately small, keyboard-first viewer for Linux.
Killer feature (from memories of using ACDSee 20 years ago when I was on Windows) - it preloads the next image while you view the current one.
